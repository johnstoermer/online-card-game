Table instructions

Make poker hands. Build a machine.

01

Choose your hand

Select one to five cards. Familiar poker patterns begin with pairs and climb through flushes and straights.

02

Score the contract

Card chips and hand chips are multiplied by the hand's multiplier. Reach the shared target before every player spends three hands.

03

Wind the echo

Play a different hand type than the previous player to raise the echo chain. Repeating a hand drops it to quiet.

04

Rebuild between rounds

Each cleared contract gives every player a permanent relic. Every third round adds a hostile house rule.
