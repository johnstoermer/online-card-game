Table instructions

Make poker hands. Dress the table.

01

Choose your hand

Select one to five cards. Sort by rank or suit, or drag cards into a custom order. Familiar poker patterns begin with pairs and climb through flushes and straights.

02

Score the contract

In Cooperative, reach the shared target. In Table Versus, every seat spends three hands and the highest score takes a win; first to three wins the match.

03

Wind the echo

Play a different hand type than the previous player to raise the echo chain. Repeating a hand drops it to quiet.

04

Dress the table

Set up to five persistent pieces on the felt. Chip stacks grow, markers rise, bells ring, and Red Felt relines the whole table. Replace one when every space is occupied.
