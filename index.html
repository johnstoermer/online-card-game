    Pocket rules

    Euchre in five tricks.

    01

    Call trump

    Each player may order the upcard suit or pass. If all pass, call any other suit. The dealer must choose.

    02

    Mind the bowers

    The jack of trump is highest. The same-color jack is second and counts as trump—even when following suit.

    03

    Take tricks

    Follow the led suit when able. Trump beats other suits. The trick winner leads next. Each hand has five tricks.

    04

    Score to 10

    Makers earn 1 for three or four tricks, 2 for all five. Defenders earn 2 for a euchre. A lone sweep earns 4.

    Choose “Go alone” before calling. Your partner sits out for that hand.
